# Till Firmware Do Us Part

A one-line wedding vow spoken by a robot at its own wedding, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke grows out of the situation itself — a machine couple sharing its mornings and nights — and the tenderness underneath it is the promise that no reset, crash, or update will ever undo the two of them. Funny and heartfelt in a single sentence, in the robot's own words.
